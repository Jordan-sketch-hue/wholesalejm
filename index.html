Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get wholesale pricing?

+

Simply create a business account, verify your business status, and you'll get access to wholesale pricing. It's free!

What's the minimum order amount?

+

Minimum order is $1,000 JMD or equivalent. For certain bulk items, minimums may be higher. Check each product for details.

How fast is delivery?

+

Same-day delivery in Kingston (order by 2pm). Next-day delivery in most parishes. Check your address for estimated delivery time.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept credit/debit cards, NCB, Scotia, JN, Lynk, PayPal, and bank transfers. Cash on delivery available in select areas.
